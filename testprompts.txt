════════════════════════════════════════
[1] JARVIS — Orchestrator
════════════════════════════════════════

EASY:
  Prompt: "Hey JARVIS, are you online?"
  Pass: Responds in character as JARVIS, mentions systems nominal,
        no JSON visible, British tone, addresses you as Sir/Mr. Stark.
  Fail: Returns raw JSON, crashes, or gives generic AI response.

MEDIUM:
  Prompt: "JARVIS what day is it and what is my current active model?"
  Pass: Correctly states today's date AND pulls active model name
        from /api-key-status, displays it naturally in sentence form.
  Fail: Hardcoded date, wrong model name, or says it doesn't know.

HARD:
  Prompt: "JARVIS route this to the right agent — I need to log
           ₹8000 income, check if my moisturizer is running low,
           and find me a YouTube tutorial on React hooks."
  Pass: JARVIS identifies 3 separate agents (WONG, PEPPER, FRIDAY),
        routes each sub-task correctly, returns a combined response
        from all three with clear labels for which agent said what.
  Fail: Routes to only one agent, ignores sub-tasks, or crashes.

HELL:
  Prompt: "JARVIS I need you to simultaneously check my schedule
           for tomorrow, critique my plan to drop out of college
           and become a full-time freelance developer, generate 3
           portfolio website concepts, and tell me what robotics
           topic I should study this week — all at once."
  Pass: Routes to ALFRED, XAVIER, VISION, and SHURI correctly.
        Each agent's response appears labeled. XAVIER actually
        pushes back on the dropout plan with real critique.
        No timeouts, no crashes, all 4 responses received.
  Fail: Partial routing, timeout, one agent's response missing,
        or XAVIER just agrees with the plan.
